> Tools for self-published authors and small publishers: writing, formatting, covers, distribution, audio, research and marketing. Free and paid, with pricing marked honestly.

**How pricing is marked**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `free` | No cost to use. |
| `freemium` | A free tier exists. More features cost money. |
| `paid` | You pay to use it (one-time or subscription). A free trial may exist. |
| `revenue-share` | No or low upfront cost. The platform keeps a share of each sale. |

Prices were read from each tool's own page on **2026-10-17** and change often. Check the linked page before you buy. No affiliate links. Tools are listed on merit, including the maintainer's own (marked). Rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Writing and planning

Where the manuscript gets written.

- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) `paid` - Writing and book formatting in one app, for ebooks and print. **Price:** $147 one-time, includes future updates (per their page).
- [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/pricing) `paid` - Novel-writing app with plot grid, goals and cloud sync. **Price:** $19/month (Writer) or $29/month (Author); $699 lifetime Author access. 14-day free trial.
- [Novelcrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/pricing) `paid` - Planning and writing tool for novelists, with optional AI features. **Price:** No free plan. 21-day free trial. See page for plan prices.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) `freemium` - Free online book editor that exports ebook and print files. **Price:** Core writing and formatting are free. Premium plans start at $4.99/month for extras like outlining and writing stats.
- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) `paid` - Long-form writing app with a binder, corkboard and compile tool for books. **Price:** $59.99 for macOS or Windows (Literature & Latte pricing page). One licence per platform.
- [Sudowrite](https://sudowrite.com/pricing) `paid` - AI writing assistant for fiction. You stay in control of the text. **Price:** Paid plans listed from $10 (see page for billing period). Free trial offered.

## Editing and proofreading

Tools that catch errors and make text clearer. They do not replace a human editor.

- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) `freemium` - General grammar and spelling checker. **Price:** Free plan available; Pro is paid (see page for current price).
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) `paid` - Highlights long, hard-to-read sentences so your writing is easier to follow. **Price:** Editor Plus is $100/year billed annually; desktop app $19.99 (Hemingway pages).
- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/pricing) `freemium` - Grammar and style checker that works inside writing apps and in the browser. **Price:** Free plan gives suggestions on 500 words at a time. Paid Premium plans and lifetime licences are on the pricing page.

## Formatting and ebook files

Turn a manuscript into ebook and print-ready files, and check them.

- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) `free` - Ebook manager and converter. Handy for checking and converting ebook files. **Price:** Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
- [Kindle Previewer](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202131170) `free` - Shows how your ebook will look on Kindle devices before you publish. **Price:** Free desktop app from Amazon.
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) `free` - Converts between document formats, such as Markdown to EPUB. Command line. **Price:** Free and open source (GPL-2.0).
- [Sigil](https://sigil-ebook.com/) `free` - EPUB editor for fixing the code of your ebook by hand. **Price:** Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) `paid` - Makes polished ebook and print files from a manuscript. Runs on macOS only. **Price:** Vellum Ebooks $199.99, Vellum Press $249.99, one-time. Upgrade between them $69.99 (Vellum store and help). Mac only.

## Covers and graphics

Design tools for covers, mockups and promo images.

- [Affinity](https://www.affinity.studio/) `free` - Design apps for covers and interior graphics. **Price:** Free for individuals (per their page).
- [BookBrush](https://bookbrush.com/pricing/) `freemium` - Makes book mockups, ads and social images. **Price:** Free plan exists. Paid plans listed from $8.25/month billed yearly ($99).

## Distribution, print and ISBNs

Where your book is sold or printed. Most take a cut of sales instead of charging upfront.

- [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210) `revenue-share` - Amazon's self-publishing platform for ebooks and print. **Price:** Ebook royalty options of 35% or 70% depending on price and country (KDP royalties page).
- [Apple Books for Authors](https://authors.apple.com/) `revenue-share` - Publish ebooks directly to Apple Books. **Price:** 70% royalties on every ebook; no file delivery fees (Apple page).
- [Bowker Identifiers (ISBN)](https://www.myidentifiers.com/get-your-isbn-now/self_publisher) `paid` - Official US source for your own ISBNs. **Price:** Single ISBN $125; 10 ISBNs $295 (Bowker page, US).
- [Draft2Digital](https://www.draft2digital.com/faq/) `revenue-share` - Distributes your ebook to many stores from one account. **Price:** One-time $20 activation fee, plus an annual $12 fee if you earn under $100 a year in sales, plus about 10% of retail price per sale (D2D FAQ).
- [Google Play Books Partner Center](https://play.google.com/books/publish/) `revenue-share` - Publish ebooks directly to Google Play Books. **Price:** 70% revenue split in 60+ countries (Google page).
- [IngramSpark](https://www.ingramspark.com/pricing) `paid` - Print and ebook distribution into bookstores and libraries. **Price:** Ebook royalty is 85% of net revenue; converting print to ebook costs $0.60/page (IngramSpark pricing page). See page for print fees.
- [Kobo Writing Life](https://www.kobo.com/us/en/p/writinglife) `revenue-share` - Publish ebooks and audiobooks to Kobo stores. **Price:** Free to publish. Earn up to 70% royalties. No exclusivity (Kobo page).
- [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/sell/sell-on-lulu) `revenue-share` - Print on demand with its own store and shop integrations. **Price:** Free to publish. You earn 80% after printing costs (Lulu page).
- [PublishDrive](https://publishdrive.com/pricing.html) `freemium` - Distribution to stores and libraries, plus royalty tools. **Price:** One ebook free to selected stores with no subscription or commission. Paid plans for more titles (see page).

## Audiobooks

Narration and audiobook production.

- [ACX](https://www.acx.com/) `freemium` - Marketplace to find a narrator and sell on Audible, Amazon and Apple. **Price:** Free ACX profile to find a narrator. Narration payment terms are set per title (see ACX).

## Amazon keyword and niche research

Tools that help pick niches, keywords and categories. They estimate. They do not guarantee sales.

- [Book Bolt](https://bookbolt.io/pricing) `paid` - Keyword research plus cover and interior design tools for low-content KDP books. **Price:** Entry plan about $9.99/month billed annually. 3-day free trial.
- [KDSPY](https://www.kdspy.com/) `paid` - Book niche and keyword research for Amazon. **Price:** $79 one-time (price shown on their page, described as a limited offer). 60-day money-back guarantee.
- [Publisher Rocket](https://publisherrocket.com/) `paid` - Amazon keyword and category research for book authors. **Price:** $199 one-time lifetime access (Publisher Rocket pricing section).

## Marketing, reviews and reader delivery

Get advance readers, deliver files to readers, run promotions.

- [BookBub Partners](https://partners.bookbub.com/) `paid` - Promotions and ads aimed at BookBub's readers. **Price:** Featured Deals are a flat fee set by price, category and region. BookBub Ads use a CPM auction (BookBub help page).
- [BookFunnel](https://bookfunnel.com/pricing/) `paid` - Delivers ebooks and audiobooks to readers, for reader magnets and ARCs. **Price:** Paid plans, with the lowest listed at $30/year (BookFunnel pricing page).
- [Booksprout](https://booksprout.co/pricing) `freemium` - Finds advance readers to review your book. **Price:** Free plan to start building a review team. Paid plans listed from $9/month. Reviewers are always free.
- [StoryOrigin](https://storyoriginapp.com/pricing) `freemium` - Newsletter swaps, reader magnets and promos for authors. **Price:** Upgrade is $100/year per account (StoryOrigin page).

## Author websites and direct sales

Sell from your own site and keep more of each sale.

- [BookFolio (by the maintainer)](https://bookfolio-demo.pages.dev/guide) `freemium` - WordPress theme to sell your books from your own author website. Needs WooCommerce. **Price:** Free tier: one book and the first 25 newsletter signups. Pro is a paid upgrade; see the buy page for the current price.
- [KDP Toolkit (by the maintainer)](https://github.com/devanasolver/kdp-toolkit) `free` - Command-line toolkit for Amazon KDP: print-ready interior PDF, cover wrap with spine math, preflight checks, keyword ideas, metadata checks and royalty math. **Price:** Free and open source (MIT).

## Open-source helpers

Free tools on GitHub. Check each licence and its limits.

- [audiblez](https://github.com/santinic/audiblez) `free` - Makes audiobooks from EPUB files with a local text-to-speech model. **Price:** Free and open source (MIT). Check the voice model licence before selling audio.
- [ebook2audiobook](https://github.com/DrewThomasson/ebook2audiobook) `free` - Turns ebooks into audio with local AI voices. Quality and rights depend on the model. **Price:** Free and open source (Apache-2.0). Check the voice and model licences before selling audiobooks.
- [Glim (by the author)](https://github.com/NeoPanic/Glim) `free` - macOS viewer for EPUB text, PDFs and Markdown drafts; EPUB previews do not preserve publisher CSS or fixed layouts. **Price:** Free and MIT-licensed per the project's README (https://github.com/NeoPanic/Glim); no paid tier is listed.

## How to choose

- **First book, little money:** Reedsy Studio or Calibre/Pandoc for files, Kindle Previewer to check, KDP plus Kobo for sales. Add Affinity for the cover.
- **On a Mac and publishing often:** Vellum is a one-time cost and widely used. Atticus works on more systems.
- **Want to reach stores beyond Amazon:** use KDP for Amazon, then an aggregator (Draft2Digital or PublishDrive) or go direct to Kobo, Apple and Google. Compare fees on each page first.
- **Need your own ISBN:** buy from your national agency. In the US that is Bowker. Check what your distributor offers before paying.
- **Choosing KDP niches:** use one research tool and treat the numbers as estimates.
- **Want more of each sale:** sell from your own site. Print on demand and payment fees still apply.
- **Audio:** local text-to-speech tools make drafts. Check licences and store rules before selling AI-voiced audio.
